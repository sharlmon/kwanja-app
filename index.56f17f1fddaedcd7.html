About Kwanja

Built for Nairobi’s tech and repair shops

A customer leaves a deposit for a screen replacement and pays the balance at pickup. Another takes a refurbished laptop on credit. A parts supplier gives you a week to pay. Kwanja keeps these amounts together.

Kwanja is for phone and computer repair shops, device retailers, and parts sellers across Nairobi, including Westlands. It works in Kenyan shillings and fits the way these shops already use WhatsApp and M-Pesa.

Questions

Common questions

Straight answers before you sign up.

Does Kwanja collect or hold money?

Payments happen through your usual channels. Kwanja records the amounts and helps you prepare reminders.

Can I track supplier debts too?

Yes. The ledger keeps money owed to you separate from money you owe. Both support partial payments.

Do I need to connect my M-Pesa account?

No. You can enter payments yourself or paste a supported confirmation SMS. You review what Kwanja extracts before a payment is recorded.

Can I download my records?

Yes. Export the ledger to CSV for your records or to open in a spreadsheet.
